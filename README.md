## The entry

A monologue of 98 words spoken by a fantasy villain, the Ashen Lord of the Nine Valleys, who has kept a hero waiting at his door. His tiredness is never announced outright: it lives in the pauses, the small domestic deflections (a wobbling step, a torch out of oil, a map left half finished), and the final request to put the dying off until tomorrow. He still has a scheme (the poison, the trapdoor, the rehearsed speech about destiny) and a history (the half-conquered valleys), so he stays a villain even while asking, in effect, for the night off.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt`. It guards the competition's limits before writing: the text must be 1–600 characters (it is 544) and about 100 words (it is 98).

```sh
node main.ts
```
