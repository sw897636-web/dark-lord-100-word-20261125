Come in. Mind the third step, it wobbles, and the torch needs oil since spring. You came to slay me, I suppose. Everyone does, eventually, usually on a Tuesday. Sit. The throne is cold, the crown pinches behind the ears, and the map of the Nine Valleys lies on the table where I left it, half conquered, half forgotten. I have the poison, the trapdoor, the speech about destiny. All rehearsed. But the candles burn low, the ravens have roosted, and honestly, hero, could we do the dying tomorrow? Tonight I would rather lock the gate and sleep.
