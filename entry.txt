Ah. The hero. Put the sword down, you'll scratch the floor; I only just had it waxed. Sit if you like. The guards are asleep; I didn't wake them. Yes, I burned your village. Your father's too, and possibly his father's. It's been a lot of villages. Forty years. The plan still stands: the eclipse, the obsidian gate, the army of the restless dead. They are very restless. I am not. The speech is written somewhere. It begins 'Tremble' and runs nine pages. Could you just assume I said it? Tremble. There. Now strike me down or pass me that blanket.
